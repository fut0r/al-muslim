# Code of conduct

Al-Muslim is built for people of every background and level of experience. Everyone taking part in
this project — in issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews — is expected to help keep it a
respectful and welcoming place.

## Expected behaviour

- Be courteous and patient. Assume good intentions.
- Give and accept feedback about the work, not the person.
- Respect differences of opinion, of school of thought and of experience.
- Be especially careful and well-sourced when discussing religious text. Disagreements about
  rulings are settled by citing reliable sources, not by argument.
- Help newcomers.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Insults, harassment, hate speech or personal attacks of any kind.
- Sectarian abuse or mockery of anyone's beliefs or practice.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Deliberately disrupting discussions.

## Enforcement

The project maintainer is responsible for enforcing this code of conduct and may remove, edit or
reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that violate it, and may block people who
repeatedly or seriously do so.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through GitHub
(<https://github.com/fut0r>). Reports are handled confidentially.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in all project spaces and whenever someone represents the project in
public.
